# cvm_rad_versions - CVM RAD: the superseded versions of DFP and ITR

**Tier:** P. **Recon date:** 2026-11-08. **Fixtures:** `tests/fixtures/cvm_rad/` (one ITR statements XML and two legacy payloads, each trimmed to a few accounts; README there).

Measured on 2026-11-08 against real responses (about 80 requests for §1 to §5, 32 for §6 and §7, 1 per 1.2 s or slower) unless marked *(owner)* or *(not verified)*. Why it exists: the open-data zips of `cvm_dfp_itr` keep only the latest version of each filing; 6,137 filings were restated and 7,558 superseded versions are in the document index, each only on RAD.

## 1. Access
- `https://www.rad.cvm.gov.br/ENETCONSULTA/frmDownloadDocumento.aspx?CodigoInstituicao=1&NumeroSequencialDocumento=<ID_DOC>`, `ID_DOC` from the `cvm_dfp_itr` index. The index's `http://` link redirects with 302 *(owner)*; the https URL answers 200 with no redirect. No auth; the generic `lastro-fin` User-Agent works. No Range support *(owner)*.
- Answer: a zip, `Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8` (so no content-type filter), 2.6 to 35 MB (mean 7.8 MB over 33 XML-era filings), 10 to 60 s to produce.
- **Busy stub:** on at least one of about 80 requests RAD answered 200 with a 319-byte HTML page (`Content-Length: 319`); the same URL worked seconds later. `min_bytes=100_000` makes the fetcher log it as absent and the next run asks again.
- Politeness: concurrency 4 and `min_interval` 0.25 s are the connector's settings; the measurements above used one request at a time.

## 2. Two layouts
- **Statements XML** (doc ids from 108951, received 2021-10-07, onward): members `<CVM6><DFP|ITR><dd-mm-yyyy>v<N>.xml` (3.1 to 22.6 MB; root `XmlDemonstracoesFinanceiras` for DFP, `XmlInformacoesTrimestraisFinanceiras` for ITR), a PDF (up to 16 MB), `DadosDocumento.xlsx`, `FormularioCadastral.xml`, `FormularioDemonstracaoFinanceira<DFP|ITR>.xml`.
- **Legacy**: every probed document up to doc 108885 (2021-10-04), and 4 of 37 sampled ones after it (109419, 112861 DFP; 115797, 109608 re-presentations of 2019/2020 periods), is a zip holding a nested `<CVM6><yyyy><mmdd><nn>.dfp|.itr` zip of entity dumps (plus the PDF, `FormularioCadastral.xml`, an `.fca`), read since 2026-11-08 (§7). Members of the nested zip (Ambev DFP 2010, doc 5110): `InfoFinaDFin.xml` 5.1 MB (one record per account line), `InformacaoFinanceiraDemonstracaoFinanceira.xml` 36 MB (the same cells, verbose, plus 3,218 empty template records), `Documento.xml`, `PeriodoDemonstracaoFinanceira.xml`, `ComposicaoCapitalSocialDemonstracaoFinanceiraNegocios.xml`, `AnexoDocumento.xml` (1.9 to 3.7 MB, the PDF), `AnexoTexto.xml`, `PagamentoProvento...`, `Resumo...`, `FormularioDemonstracaoFinanceira<DFP|ITR>.xml`. All 23 legacy downloads fetched had this member set. The connector asks for every superseded version (7,558); a nested zip without the four kept members is archived as a `legacy-unread` JSON marker and counted under `cvm_rad_versions/not_data`.

## 3. What is kept
**XML layout.** `extract_statements` keeps the statements XML with the text of every `<ImagemObjetoArquivoPdf>` removed (the element stays); the zip, the PDF and the other members are not kept. Raw holds that XML plus a JSON record (full sha256 and size of the zip, each member's name, size, CRC-32). Sizes over 33 filings: member 5.0 MB mean (3.1 to 22.6), kept 1.77 MB mean (35 %), zstd of the kept XML 31 KB mean, about 0.4 % of the 7.8 MB downloaded. `ResponsavelDocumento` (filer name, phone, e-mail) is never parsed; it was empty in all 34 XMLs seen.

**Legacy layout.** `extract_legacy` keeps four members of the nested zip byte for byte, in a stored (uncompressed, fixed-date) zip with extension `szip` (listed in `store.raw.COMPRESSIBLE`, so zstd compresses the members together): `Documento.xml`, `PeriodoDemonstracaoFinanceira.xml`, `ComposicaoCapitalSocialDemonstracaoFinanceiraNegocios.xml`, `InfoFinaDFin.xml`. Dropped: PDF and annexes, notes, dividends, summary, forms, and the 30 to 80 MB `InformacaoFinanceiraDemonstracaoFinanceira.xml` (a duplicate of `InfoFinaDFin.xml`: 810 of its 4,028 records are populated, the same 810 accounts, each repeating the filing header). The record lists the outer members and the nested ones (name, size, CRC-32), `kept` and `dropped`. Over 23 filings: download 2.1 to 15.8 MB (mean 7.8), kept 1.9 to 9.0 MB (mean 5.5), **zstd (level 9) 10.5 to 29.8 KB (mean 21.4), 0.27 % of the download**: the same tens of KB as the XML era. Extraction plus parse takes 0.2 to 0.7 s.

## 4. XML to `statement_line` (the open-data contract, `cvm_statements.STATEMENT_LINE`)
- `EscalaMoeda` 1 UNIDADE, 2 MIL; `Moeda` 1 REAL. CNPJ digits to `00.000.000/0000-00`; `CodigoCvm` 6 digits. `ContaFixa` True/False to `fixed_account`. Statements: `BalancoPatrimonialAtivo/Passivo` BPA/BPP, `DemonstracaoResultado` DRE, `...Abrangente` DRA, `DemonstracaoFluxoCaixa` DFC_MI/DFC_MD by each account's `Metodo`, `...MutacoesPatrimonioLiquido` DMPL, `...ValorAdicionado` DVA; `DfConsolidadas` con, `DfIndividuais` ind.
- **DFP:** `UltimoExercicio`/`PenultimoExercicio` are ÚLTIMO/PENÚLTIMO (`AntepenultimoExercicio`, a third DMPL block: not in open data, not read); flows carry `DtInicio/DtFim...ExercicioSocial`, balance sheets only the end date.
- **ITR:** balance sheet `TrimestreAtual` (end `DtFimTrimestreAtual`) and `ExercicioAnterior` (end of last fiscal year). DRE/DRA: `TrimestreAtual` and `AcumuladoExercicioAtual` are both ÚLTIMO (quarter and year to date), `TrimestreAnterior`/`AcumuladoExercicioAnterior` both PENÚLTIMO, the prior-year spans being the same dates a year earlier (a month end stays a month end). DFC/DVA: year to date only (`AcumuladoAtualExercicio`). In Q1 the quarter is the year to date: one line.
- Numbers are pt-BR: an optional decimal comma (`4,14`) and, in any statement, dots grouping thousands (`-7.626.000`); anything else is refused. Empty value elements and unused template slots (no description, 0) produce no line.
- `DS_CONTA`: open data's text is the XML's with line breaks and tabs removed, ASCII spaces stripped (before the breaks go), no-break spaces kept, U+2013 as `-`, cut at 100 characters. The parser reproduces that; the full text stays in raw.
- DMPL columns are elements (`CapitalSocialIntegralizado`, `ReservasCapital`..., `PatrimonioLiquidoConsolidado`) or, in some filings, `DadosContas/CamposDadosContas` with a `Descricao`; each keeps its own name (labels as in open data, plus `Reservas de Reavaliação` and `Ajustes de Avaliação Patrimonial`).
- `ComposicaoCapital` gives `share_capital`, counts as written (thousands when `EscalaQtdAcoes` is 2, as open data carries them; an absent treasury count is 0).

## 5. Verification against open data (33 filings)
Filings whose open-data version equals the RAD version (version 1 to 3, the latest), 14 DFP and 19 ITR, doc 109079 to 160878 (reference dates 2020-12 to 2026-06), industrials, banks, insurers, securitisers; 52,032 open-data lines compared on (form, cnpj, reference_date, version, statement, consolidation, period_order, period_start, period_end, equity_column, account_code). 2011 to 2021-09 filings are legacy-layout and could not be compared.
- **Value, name and key: 30 of 33 filings match 100 %** (52,032 lines, 51,769 matched); share counts match in 33 of 33. DS_CONTA matches on every line.
- **DMPL: open data labels the Nth column of a filing with the Nth label of a fixed list.** Where a filing's columns differ from that list (an extra `ReservasReavaliacao`, `AjustesAvaliacaoPatrimonial` before `LucroPrejuizoAcumulado`, a ninth column) the open-data `equity_column` is shifted (e.g. Bradesco 2023-Q1 consolidated: its `Patrimônio Líquido` is the OCI value -3,104,199; the real one, 158,265,358, is under `Participação dos Não Controladores`), and a ninth column gets a null label. Relabelling the XML columns by position reproduces open data exactly for the 6- and 7-column layouts (10 filings); the 9-column layout (3 filings: 125759, 132207, 160511; 263 lines, 95 to 97 % of their lines) also orders its last three columns differently. This parser keeps the element's own name, so those lines differ from open data by design; they are dq findings about open data, not about this parser.
- `ST_CONTA_FIXA`: 410 of 52,032 lines differ; 388 are the EPS headings `3.99`, `3.99.01`, `3.99.02` (XML `True`, open data `N`), the rest are accounts of the new CMN 4.966 bank plan. The XML flag is kept.
- Empty EPS headings (`3.99`...) with priced lines below are 0 in open data and here; with nothing below, absent in both.
- `CodigoCvm` and company name can differ from open data (open data uses the current registry values; e.g. `MAHLE-METAL LEVE` vs `MAHLE METAL LEVE`, a securitiser with another `CD_CVM`); the XML's are kept, neither is a key.
- The XML holds statements open data lacks: Aura Minerals 2026-Q2 has 524 individual-statement lines absent from open data (compatible with the "5 index keys without individual statements" of `cvm_dfp_itr`).

## 6. Legacy layout to `statement_line`
Matched against open data (§7); the zip carries codes, not labels.
- **Header:** `Documento.xml`: `CodigoTipoDocumento` 3 ITR, 4 DFP; `DataReferenciaDocumento`, `NumeroVersaoDocumento`, `CodigoEscalaMoeda` 1 UNIDADE / 2 MIL, `CodigoMoeda` 1 REAL, `CompanhiaAberta` (`CodigoCvm`, `NumeroCnpjCompanhiaAberta`, name). The files are UTF-8; accents are intact.
- **Lines:** one `InfoFinaDFin` record per account: `PlanoConta/NumeroConta`, `IndicadorContaFixa`, `DescricaoConta1`, `ValorConta1..12`, and `PlanoConta/VersaoPlanoConta`: `CodigoTipoDemonstracaoFinanceira` 2 BPA, 3 BPP, 4 DRE, 5 DRA, 6 DFC_MD (not seen in the sample; by the sequence), 7 DFC_MI, 8 DMPL, 9 DVA; `CodigoTipoInformacaoFinanceira` 1 individual, 2 consolidated.
- **Periods:** `ValorContaN` is period id N of `PeriodoDemonstracaoFinanceira.xml` (id, start, end). DFP: 1 this fiscal year, 2 the last. ITR: 2 the quarter, 3 the last fiscal year, 4 year to date, 5 the quarter a year earlier, 6 year to date a year earlier; the balance sheet uses 2 and 3, DRE and DRA 2, 4, 5, 6, DFC and DVA 4 and 6. `period_order` ÚLTIMO when the period ends at the reference date, PENÚLTIMO when it ends a year earlier or at the last fiscal year end; a balance sheet has no start. A column is used when it is in that template or has a non-zero value; open data lists zeros (all-zero periods, accounts with zeros). A first-quarter ITR has the quarter and the year to date over the same dates: one line (a second non-zero value for the same key is an error).
- **DMPL:** a record carries its block's period id in its own `PeriodoDemonstracaoFinanceira`, and `ValorContaN` is equity column N (width 6 to 8, from the last non-zero column of any block of the statement). The zip does not name the columns; open data labels the Nth with the Nth entry of a fixed list (Capital Social Integralizado, Reservas de Capital..., Reservas de Lucro, Lucros ou Prejuízos Acumulados, Outros Resultados Abrangentes, Patrimônio Líquido, Participação dos Não Controladores, Patrimônio Líquido Consolidado), and the parser does the same (a ninth column would get no label). That is right for the usual 6 and 8 column layouts, and an assumption for others (a 7-column individual DMPL, Itaú 2021, matches open data, which is positional too).
- **`DS_CONTA`** as in §4. **Shares:** the capital composition file, counts as written (open data has `share_capital` only from 2020).

## 7. Verification of the legacy parse (23 filings)
Latest versions in open data, received before 2021-10-07, doc 5110 to 103318: 12 DFP and 11 ITR, reference dates 2010-12 to 2021-03: Ambev, Embraer, Equatorial, Porto Seguro (insurer), Qualicorp, Localiza Fleet, Petrobras, Itaú, Santander (banks), BTG securitisers and holding. 41,491 open-data lines compared on the §5 key.
- **Key, value and `DS_CONTA`: 41,491 of 41,491 match** (no line missing, no value or name different) on 22 filings; the 23rd (Santander ITR 2018-Q2, doc 76644) also matches and has 264 extra lines, its consolidated DMPL block for 2017-H1, which is in the filing and absent from open data (a gap of open data). Share counts match on the 4 filings from 2020 (open data has none before).
- **`ST_CONTA_FIXA`: 418 of 41,491 lines differ (1.0 %):** 318 are the EPS headings `3.99`, `3.99.01`, `3.99.02` (legacy flag True, open data N, as in the XML era), 92 are legacy False / open data True (for example DRE `3.01.01`, `3.01.02`, `3.02.01` of a bank plan, DFC `6.01.01`), 8 the reverse. The filing's flag is kept; open data's looks derived from the account code, not from the filing.
- The first fit of the periods used only non-zero columns and lost every all-zero period (a DRA of a securitiser, a DVA of a bank) and the March-only quarter column that Porto Seguro entered in its 2015-Q1 ITR (open data lists it with zeros): hence the template.

## 8. Ephemerality and open items
Not ephemeral (RAD keeps every version). Open: the legacy backfill itself (5,605 versions, ~44 GB of download for ~140 MB kept); DFC method direct (code 6) and a DMPL with more than 8 columns were not seen in the 23 filings and are untested against real files; the doc-id boundary of the XML era (108885 / 108951, 65 ids unprobed) no longer matters, the connector reads both layouts.

## 9. Backfill complete (2026-11-09)
- **Every superseded version RAD serves is held,** all but document 67723 (an ITR v1). RAD answers that one with HTTP 200 and its own .NET error ("Index was out of range…", 109 bytes) on every try.
- **Under load, RAD answers HTTP 200 with a "busy" page.** It was read as absent until 2026-11-08, which had dropped 3,372 versions; it is now retried.
- **Two runs died of MemoryError** when another process on the host held about 20 GB of RAM; the retry passes completed the backfill.
- **Layouts met in the backfill:**
  - repeated capital compositions with a field left at 0 in one copy (merged field by field);
  - "Total do Patrimônio Líquido" DMPL labels;
  - a statements XML named with a one-digit hour ("0_00_00").
- **Reparse:** 12,561,077 statement lines and 7,469 share-capital rows (4 quarantined). Staging compacted from 95,284
  files (989 MB) to 848 (65 MB).
- **Left unparsed, 21 of 7,473 versions (raw kept):**
  - most are filings whose two periods share both start and end date while their values differ: the filing's own
    period metadata is broken;
  - 2 values typed with a point decimal ("-0.05"): refused, never guessed;
  - 1 DMPL column label not seen before;
  - 1 filing with no start date for the year before last.
