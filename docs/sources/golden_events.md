## Lessons for the model
- A B3 `BONIFICACAO` factor is a **percent**, `GRUPAMENTO` a **fraction**; read with the label. Ratios from percent: 3 % = 103:100, 12.5 % = 9:8, 2 % = 51:50, 40 % = 7:5.
- **The B3 backend is not complete for stock events** (ITUB 2025-03, SLCE 2023-04, RADL 2023-04 missing): the CVM-IPE route and the price-gap test of PLAN §4.7.3 are required, not optional.
- The B3 data-com is the last day with the right; the ex-date differs by trading calendar (ITUB 23->26 Dec, GRND 26->29 Dec, SLCE 30 Dec -> 2 Jan) and comes from the filing.
- Merger/ticker/ISIN changes are absent from B3 events; they must come from COTAHIST (ticker and ISIN per day) and IPE.
- 4 of 16 first IPE Link_Download requests returned an empty HTML shell (4.3 KB); a retry returned the PDF (see cvm_ipe.md).
